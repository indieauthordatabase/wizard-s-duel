Card table for four

One table, four seats. Scroll down to your seat: each tabletop is turned 90° from the one above it. Cards you drop into your hand stay hidden from everyone else.

How to play with it
  • Drag a card to move it. Drop it onto another card to stack it (hold Ctrl or ⌘ while dropping to overlap without snapping).
  • Double-click or double-tap a card to flip it. Hover over a card to see it larger.
  • Shift-drag, or switch on Pile drag, to move a whole pile at once.
  • Right-click or press and hold a card for more: flip or shuffle a pile, spread it out, or take a card into your hand.
  • Drag cards into the hand strip at the bottom of your tabletop to hide them from the others. Double-click a card in your hand to play it face up.
  • Deal sends cards from the top of the table pile to every hand. Gather stacks all table cards in the center, Shuffle also mixes them face down, and Reset returns every card, hands included, to the center.
